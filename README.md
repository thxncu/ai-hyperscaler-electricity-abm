## Overview

This repository implements an agent-based model (ABM) of a wholesale electricity market populated by 5 hyperscaler firms, 10 incumbent utilities, and 40 independent power producers (IPPs), scaled to mid-sized U.S. ISO conditions (≈25 GW average load, ≈18 million MWh per month). Three candidate mechanisms through which AI hyperscaler entry may restructure competition are implemented as independently togglable parameters:

- **M1 (compute → forecasting precision)** — computational advantage reduces bidding forecast error
- **M2 (capital → capacity lock-up)** — cash-rich hyperscalers acquire distressed IPP assets and execute long-term offtake agreements
- **M3 (private load information)** — behind-the-meter co-location grants additional bid-side information

The independent togglability supports a 2³ factorial design crossed with six market scenarios, sensitivity grids, CAISO 2025 external validation, and counterfactual policy simulations with 7-level dose-response sweeps. The combined design comprises approximately 18,400 model runs.

## Quick start

After cloning the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/[username]/ai-hyperscaler-electricity-abm.git
cd ai-hyperscaler-electricity-abm
pip install -r requirements.txt
python baseline_model.py --test
```

The self-test (10 routines) should complete in roughly 5 seconds and report all tests passing.

For a quick scenario-sweep demo (3 Monte Carlo replications × 24 months, completes in approximately 1 second):

```bash
python run_scenarios.py --quick
```

## Mechanism toggles

| Mechanism | Activation parameter | Default | Effect when active |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 (compute → forecasting) | `beta` | 0.4 | Reduces forecast error σ proportional to relative compute share |
| M2 (capital → capacity lock-up) | `theta_DA` | $360M (scaled) | Hyperscalers acquire distressed IPP assets via real-options trigger |
| M3 (private load information) | `delta_sigma_reduction` | 0 (off) | Additional σ reduction for AI firms with co-location agreements |

Setting `beta = 0`, `theta_DA = inf`, or `delta_sigma_reduction = 0` disables the corresponding mechanism. The 2³ factorial in `analysis.py` automates the eight toggle combinations.

## Scenario codes

Six empirically coherent scenarios are defined in `baseline_model.py:SCENARIOS_6CELL`:

| Code | Compute distribution | PPA penetration | Regulatory timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| LLL | Dispersed | Low | Ex-post |
| LLH | Dispersed | Low | Ex-ante |
| LHH | Dispersed | High | Ex-ante |
| HLH | Concentrated | Low | Ex-ante |
| HHL | Concentrated | High | Ex-post |
| HHH | Concentrated | High | Ex-ante |

Two combinations (LHL, HLL) are excluded as empirically incoherent — compute dispersion paired with PPA concentration (or vice versa) does not appear in the 2024–2025 transaction record summarized in `empirical_anchor.py`.

## Headline results (reported in the manuscript)

- M2 dominates structural concentration universally: HHI effect +166 to +190 across all six scenarios (median +178, all p < 0.001).
- The M1 effect on the AI Lerner index operates within a bounded regime defined by AI compute share, with active range 0.60–0.80 (effects +0.021 to +0.031) and null effects below 0.50 or above 0.90.
- Compute–capital synergy is conditional, emerging significantly only under concentrated compute (strongest in HHH +47).
- External validation against CAISO 2025 places three of five model metrics within empirical range. Clearing-price volatility and IPP attrition are flagged for recalibration, with limitations discussed in the manuscript.
- Counterfactual dose-response identifies minimum effective doses for break-up (intervention share 0.60), non-AI PPA disclosure (share 0.40), and lock-up indicator (HHI trigger 1,100), with a transparent null for §203 cost-disclosure.

## Data

### CAISO 2025 day-ahead market data

Used in `caiso_validation.py` for external validation (Table 4, Figure 2). The raw data are publicly available through California ISO OASIS at http://oasis.caiso.com. The script `caiso_validation.py` documents the download protocol (SCE DLAP, 15-minute granularity, 35,040 observations annually). Raw CAISO data are not redistributed in this repository per OASIS terms.

### Hyperscaler transaction database

Eight major 2024–2025 hyperscaler energy deals (Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Meta) are compiled in `empirical_anchor.py:TRANSACTIONS` from SEC filings, company press releases, FERC dockets, and verified trade press. Sources are listed in the source column of each record. The aggregate buyer-side Herfindahl–Hirschman index of the sample (5,034) confirms the empirical buyer concentration motivating the H-compute scenarios in the model.

### Hyperscaler cash reserves

Year-end 2023 cash and short-term investments for the five major U.S. hyperscalers (Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Oracle) are summarized in `empirical_anchor.py:HYPERSCALER_CASH`, totaling approximately USD 385 billion. This figure anchors the M2 acquisition trigger (θ_DA).

## Known limitations

These are stated in the manuscript and reflected in the model implementation.

1. **Closed-cohort IPP modeling**: The model tracks the initial 40 IPPs through the simulation horizon without permitting new entry. This contributes to the observed IPP attrition gap relative to the CAISO empirical baseline (53.9 percent vs 26.2 percent at 120 months).
2. **Single-zone topology**: No transmission constraints or multiple bidding zones. A multi-zone extension is a priority for follow-on work.
3. **Pay-as-bid clearing**: Implemented in v6 for robustness testing (Table S4). The linear markup specification is calibrated for uniform-price clearing and is not optimal under price discrimination; a strategic supply-function-equilibrium implementation is warranted for substantive M3 testing under pay-as-bid.
4. **CF3 mechanism**: The §203 cost-disclosure counterfactual abstracts to narrowing the cross-acquirer cost-multiplier distribution, which is an inadequate proxy for the behavioral mechanism through which disclosure operates. A behavioral model of acquirer learning under disclosure is required to test the policy substantively.
